1. EVALUATION CYCLE
- Q1 Goal Setting (Completed by Feb 15)
- Mid-Year Performance Check-in (Completed by July 15)
- End-of-Year Formal Assessment & 360 Feedback (Completed by Dec 1)

2. CORE COMPETENCY BENCHMARKS
- Technical Excellence and Job Execution (40%)
- Cross-Functional Collaboration & Communication (25%)
- Problem Solving and Ownership (20%)
- Adherence to Security and Corporate Ethics (15%)

3. RATING SCALE
- Level 5: Consistently Exceeds High Expectations
- Level 4: Frequently Exceeds Expectations
- Level 3: Meets Expectations Solidly
- Level 2: Needs Targeted Improvement
- Level 1: Unsatisfactory Performance (Triggers Performance Improvement Plan)
