1. COMMITMENT TO INTEGRITY
Every employee, contractor, and executive is expected to maintain the highest
standards of professional and personal integrity in all corporate activities.

2. DATA PRIVACY & CLOUD SECURITY
- Never share corporate login credentials or Multi-Factor Authentication codes.
- Only upload departmental documentation to your designated storage area.
- Shared documents must contain general corporate information, standard operating
  procedures, or public company announcements.

3. WHISTLEBLOWER & ETHICS HOTLINE
Employees who observe potential compliance violations, unauthorized data access,
or security breaches can report anonymously via:
- Online Portal: ethics.internal.corp
- Secure Phone: 1-800-CORP-ETHICS
