Document Title: Enterprise Network & Zero-Trust VPN Access Guidelines
Document ID:    IT-DOC-2026-003
Classification: Internal IT Standard
Author:         Network Infrastructure Lead
Last Updated:   2026-01-28

1. SCOPE & OBJECTIVE
Defines mandatory protocols for remote and on-premise connection to corporate cloud
VPCs, internal knowledge bases, and production database clusters.

2. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA)
- Hardware security keys (FIDO2 / YubiKey) or TOTP authenticator apps are required.
- SMS-based OTP is deprecated and rejected due to SIM-swapping vulnerabilities.

3. SPLIT-TUNNELING RESTRICTION
- Split-tunneling is strictly disabled for sessions touching S3 storage repositories.
- DNS queries are routed through encrypted corporate DNS resolvers with threat filtering.

4. SESSION TIMEOUTS
- Idle session timeout: 30 minutes.
- Absolute maximum session duration: 12 hours before mandatory re-authentication.
