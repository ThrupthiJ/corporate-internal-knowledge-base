1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
This document outlines standard human resource policies, employee benefits,
workplace conduct, working hours, and grievance redressal mechanisms for all
corporate personnel.

2. EMPLOYMENT CATEGORIES & WORK HOURS
- Core business hours: 09:00 - 18:00 (Monday through Friday).
- Flexible work arrangements are subject to department head approval.
- Remote work stipend is processed bi-annually through HR payroll.

3. LEAVE ENTITLEMENTS
- Annual Paid Time Off (PTO): 22 days per calendar year.
- Sick & Casual Leave: 10 days.
- Parental Leave: 16 weeks paid primary caregiver leave.

4. EQUAL EMPLOYMENT OPPORTUNITY (EEO)
The corporation provides equal employment opportunities to all employees and
applicants without regard to race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age,
disability, or genetics.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
Salary structures, employee grievance reports, and personal identifying
information (PII) must be stored exclusively in the HR secure storage folder.
Unauthorized transfer or dissemination is grounds for immediate termination.
