1. VALUE PROPOSITIONS TO HIGHLIGHT
- Granular Folder-Level Security: Keep HR salaries, IT infrastructure specs,
  and Sales pipelines completely segregated under zero-trust S3 policies.
- Instant Cross-Department Search: Find public and approved knowledge items in
  sub-50 milliseconds without risking permission leakage.
- Complete Audit Trail Compliance: Satisfy GDPR, HIPAA, and SOC 2 requirements
  with immutable tamper-evident logs of every read, write, and blocked attempt.

2. OBJECTION HANDLING QUICK REFERENCE
- Objection: "Can our auditors see who tried to download confidential files?"
  Response: "Yes, every unauthorized request generates an immediate 403 alert
  in CloudWatch and our central audit trail."
- Objection: "Is deployment locked into one cloud provider?"
  Response: "The application supports both AWS S3 object storage and standard
  S3-compatible APIs for high flexibility and multi-cloud readiness."
