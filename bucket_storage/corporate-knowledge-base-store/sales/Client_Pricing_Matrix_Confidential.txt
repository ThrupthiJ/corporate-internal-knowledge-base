1. BASE PRICING TIERS
- Tier 1 (Up to 250 Users): $18 / user / month billed annually.
- Tier 2 (251 - 1,000 Users): $14 / user / month billed annually.
- Tier 3 (1,001 - 5,000 Users): $10 / user / month + $5,000 dedicated cloud setup.
- Tier 4 (Enterprise Unlimited): Custom contract ($100k minimum floor).

2. DISCOUNTING THRESHOLDS & APPROVAL MATRIX
- Up to 10% Discount: Account Executive discretion.
- 11% - 20% Discount: Sales Director sign-off required in CRM.
- 21% - 30% Discount: VP of Sales formal written authorization.
- Greater than 30%: CFO approval required.
